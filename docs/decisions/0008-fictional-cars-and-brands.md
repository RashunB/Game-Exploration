# 0008: Fictional cars and brands only

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-23

## Context

The game is about cars and will sell on Steam.

## Decision

All cars, brands, and logos are fictional: no real manufacturer names, badges, or recognizable body designs.

## Alternatives

- Licensed real cars: not recorded as considered.

## Consequences

Stand-in and City Sample vehicle meshes need to be checked for real badges or recognizable designs before anything ships. The hero car needs a fictional name (`PLAN.md` section 11).

## Revisit if

Not applicable unless a licensing deal is on the table.

## Sources

`PLAN.md` section 2 states the rule without a reason. The usual reason is trademark and trade-dress risk on a commercial release, which makes manufacturer licensing necessary for real cars; that reason is not written down for this project. Rashun: confirm or correct.
