## What was tested

Driving the hero car (vehicle template SportsCar stand-in) around the greybox takeover ring, and whether drifts, donuts, and the combo score.

## What felt wrong, session by session

1. First playtest (`8853f62`): drifts felt stiff, like a loose turn. Speed felt low, and the handbrake felt too strong.
2. Second (`40f1255`): scoring worked, but drifts would not hold and turning felt stiff. The template steering suits a road car.
3. Third, first with telemetry (`a389d39`): the car never left 1st gear (RPM pinned at the 6000 upshift point, 49 km/h top speed). The rear wheels spun constantly and the fronts slid while the rears held: understeer, zero drift samples.
4. Code fix, no new playtest (`b4b664e`): rotation damping meant for the air was applied on the ground every frame, because Chaos vehicles never report `IsMovingOnGround`. It resisted all yaw, so the car's slip angle stayed at 1 to 2 degrees with the rears skidding. Fixed by asking the wheels for ground contact.
5. Ring reshaped (`b2c97d7`): the 50 m circle had no straight long enough to leave 1st gear, so the crowd ring became a 120 x 70 m oval.
6. First drive in the dressed city (`7f8d344`): drifting worked (19 drift and 9 donut samples, score 175). The car still never left 1st, because RPM peaked at 5958 at 51 km/h, 42 RPM short of the shift point, when the straight ran out.
